# Echo of the Void - Changelog v2.1.0

Date: 2026-10-08

Biggest update since 2.0. It also includes the 2.0.1 hotfix.

Details are kept vague on purpose, most of this is better found in game.

## New content

- Several new creatures. Some of them hunt you, some only want to be seen, and one of them can take you somewhere you won't like.
- Caves, deep water and the Nether are a bit less empty than before.
- New events, and a few old ones reworked from scratch.
- A lot of small new behaviours for regular mobs.
- Original music for some of the bigger moments.

## Combat

- Hostile creatures now take your gear into account. Better weapons and armour won't make them trivial anymore, but a fight stays winnable if you play it well.
- Most of them can actually be hit now. Some still won't stay around long enough to find out.
- Creatures no longer keep acting while they die.

## Balancing and polish

- The heavier events happen less often, and hit harder when they do.
- The mod's music follows your sound settings, so you'll still hear it with Minecraft music turned down, without it blasting over everything else.
- Messages now show up after the thing they're about, not before.
- Some sounds were repeating way too often. They now leave you some silence.
- Creature names no longer show up in death messages, subtitles or info overlay mods.
- Fake lightning is now purely visual: no fire, no damage, no broken blocks.
- Performance pass on the heaviest scenes, and fewer particles in some weather.

## Fixes

- Events could stay paused for a long time after restarting a world (2.0.1 fix).
- Some encounters could start without anything actually showing up.
- The "Experimental Settings" warning no longer appears because of this mod.
- Lots of pathfinding, water, ledge and save/load fixes.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.219+
- Java 21
- Client and dedicated server, same file on both.
- Worlds from 1.1.1, 2.0.0 and 2.0.1 stay compatible. No ID, command, config key, network protocol or save data key was removed or renamed.
- Back up your worlds before updating.
