# Echo of the Void - Changelog v2.3.0

Date: 2026-10-11

The journals update. Details are kept vague on purpose, the rest is better found in game.

## New content

- The journals were reworked from scratch. Each one now waits in its own place, somewhere out there, and they turn up one at a time, in the order of the story.
- Every place belongs to the person who wrote the journal. Some of them are small. Some of them are not.
- You won't have to search blindly for the first one. Keep an eye on your chests.
- The further the story goes, the further you will have to travel.
- Old structures no longer hold journals, but they still have a few things to give you.

## Multiplayer

- Journals are now shared by the whole world. When one player finds one, the story moves on for everyone.

## Balancing and polish

- A journal lost by accident finds its way back to where it was. Throwing one away on purpose is another story.
- Journals can now be read on a lectern like any other book.

## Fixes

- Creatures now fully ignore players in creative and spectator mode, including the ones that only watch.
- A small event could stay stuck when you looked at it from the side.
- Lots of small fixes.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.219+
- Java 21
- Client and dedicated server, same file on both.
- Worlds from 2.2.1 and earlier stay compatible. Journals already found still count, for everyone in the world, and the next one will show up in its own place. No ID, command, config key, network protocol or save data key was removed or renamed.
- Back up your worlds before updating.
