# Echo of the Void - Changelog v2.2.1

Date: 2026-10-10

A bugfix and balancing update. Details are kept vague on purpose.

## Combat

- Every creature that fights you now scales with your gear the same way. A few of them were still way too easy to kill.
- Some hunters no longer give up halfway through an attack.
- Something you meet on the water might not stay in the water anymore.
- Most creatures swim a lot better now. Jumping in a lake is not a free escape anymore.
- Something down there wants your air.

## Balancing and polish

- One of the big events is a bit shorter.
- Something that stared at you from up close for way too long now keeps its distance.
- Creature cries now come from the creature and follow it as it moves.
- An old acquaintance knows how to eat, climbs properly now, and a wall will not keep it out for long.

## Fixes

- Creative mode players are no longer hunted or dragged into anything.
- A big event could leave a second copy of its creature behind.
- One creature would never come back for you after your first encounter.
- A hidden event could be missed for good if something else was going on at the time.
- Lots of small AI fixes.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.219+
- Java 21
- Client and dedicated server, same file on both. Both sides need 2.2.1: the client and the server now share one more kind of sound message.
- Worlds from 2.2.0 and earlier stay compatible. No ID, command, config key or save data key was removed or renamed.
- Back up your worlds before updating.
